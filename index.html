About

I trained as an architect at the Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur (B.Arch Hons., 2021–2026, CGPA 8.31/10) and moved into machine learning through research at Universiti Malaya, OCAD University (Mitacs Globalink) and the National University of Singapore. I now work as a product engineer at SLnko Energy, where I build floor-plan automation and document-intelligence tools in Python.

The thread through my work: take a messy spatial or regulatory problem from architecture and planning, and turn it into something a program can check, score or generate.
