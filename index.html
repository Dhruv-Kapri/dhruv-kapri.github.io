About

I hold a Bachelor of Architecture from the Indian Institute of Technology, Kharagpur (2021-2026). I work at the boundary of architecture and machine learning, and I am currently a Product Engineer at SLnko Energy, where I build floorplan automation and document-intelligence tools in Python. Earlier research took me to OCAD University (Mitacs Globalink), Universiti Malaya and the National University of Singapore.

Research interests

  • Spatial and geospatial AI
  • Constraint-based design generation
  • Large language models for architecture, engineering and construction (AEC) documents

Selected projects

  • GIS-Driven Rooftop Solar Potential, Washington DC (code). City-scale Python pipeline (LiDAR surface model, GRASS r.sun radiation, RANSAC roof planes, PVWatts yield) scoring 100,064 DC rooftops, with a public MapLibre and PMTiles web map. Specific yield on a Glover Park benchmark was about 1% off Esri's tutorial (1162 vs 1150).
  • Rhodes wildfire analysis. Learning project applying raster and vector analysis in Python to the 2023 Rhodes (Greece) wildfire, using Sentinel-2 imagery, a digital elevation model and OpenStreetMap layers.
  • Distributed job queue. A job queue built from scratch in Python, exploring workers, retries, dead-letter queues, concurrency and fault-tolerant task processing.
